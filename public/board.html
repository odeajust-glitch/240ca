How to get alerts on this phone

iPhone: first, put the site on your Home Screen

An iPhone only sends alerts from the Home Screen icon, not from Safari.

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open the site from the new icon, sign in, and turn on your alerts there.

Android: add the icon

  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  3. Alerts work from Chrome or from the icon.

Then turn on alerts for your turn

  1. Type your turn above (for example SR03), or your name code (see below), and pick it.
  2. A box opens. Tick what you want to hear about: lined up, ordered, on duty, and so on.
  3. Tap OK. When the phone asks, tap Allow notifications.
  4. Your turn then shows under the box with a 🔔. Tap it any time to change or stop its alerts.

Follow yourself with a name code

A name code follows a person, not a turn, so your alerts move with you when you change turns or work off the spareboard.

  1. Your code is the first two letters of your first name, then the first two of your last name. John Smith is JOSM.
  2. Type it in the box above, the same as a turn.
  3. Pick 👤 your code (alerts move with their turn). The other choice follows only the turn you are on today.
  4. Choose your alerts and tap OK, as above.

The code also works in Turn History and Analytics, to see your own trips. A code that isn't found yet means you haven't been seen on a board since the site started watching.

Not getting alerts? Check the phone's Settings → Notifications for this site, and that Do Not Disturb or a Focus is off.
