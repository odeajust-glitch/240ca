Except as provided by paragraph 18.7, employees in freight service will not be held at other than the home terminal longer than 14 hours except that for an employee who books in excess of 3 hours rest pursuant to sub-paragraph 51.11 (b) hereof, the 14-hour period shall commence at the expiration of such rest.
