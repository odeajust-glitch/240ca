    Who's available at call time

    Everyone on the list, in seniority order. Statuses start as CATS showed them on 13 Nov. For a real case, set each person as they were then: off, on rest until a time (rest booked past the call: skipped), and, for overtime, when their last yard shift ended and how many yard shifts they had worked since Monday. The grey line under each person comes from their job's own schedule; fill the boxes in when you know better.

    Examples

      Rules

      Each step takes the most senior person who fits it. A step can be switched off, moved or changed; the answer and the examples' score update as you go. Changes are a draft until saved.

      Engineers (qualified yardmasters working as locomotive engineers) are reached only in the canvass, after everyone else. That is a hard rule, so no step can be set to them.

      People called in a different group

      By default each person's group comes from their job (YA job: regular yardmaster; EXYA01: the spare; conductor or yard job: 4.16; engineer job: canvass only). Change one here if it is wrong.

      Tries the groups in every order, both call layouts, the own-shift check and its hours, and the 4.16 step with and without road conductors. The rest, overtime, engineer and call-lead rules stay as they are.
      Saved versions

      Where this comes from. The list: CATS Train Service 3 → 1, list code 3G YA, district 45 SA, read 13 Nov 2026 (34 people; names as CATS shows them). The starting rules: the Sarnia calling procedures (TCRC-CTY, 2011), "Relief – Yardmasters", with the rules confirmed 13 Nov 2026: anyone on rest when the call is made is skipped; if nobody is on the spareboard, anyone working in the yard that day who hasn't been called yet gets it first (moved up from their own shift); straight-time steps pass over anyone on overtime, so someone in the yard at straight time comes before the regular yardmasters on their rest day; engineers only in the canvass, last. The rest is not yet confirmed. Shifts: the yardmaster and yard jobs' start times, the crews' days off and the tower's 2-on 2-off 3-on rotation, as worked out from the board checks of 24 Oct to 12 Nov 2026, not from a posted sheet.

      Set by hand for now. Where road and spare turns are at call time, their rest, and the spare yardmaster's last yard shift and shifts this week. Temporary vacancies are still to come; the "Working at call time" box is there until then.
